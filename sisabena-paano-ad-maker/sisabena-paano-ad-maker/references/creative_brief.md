# Creative Brief — Sisabena Paano 30-Second Ad

## Goal

Create a stunning, professional 30-second video advertisement for Sisabena Paano, using the website `https://sisabena-paano.vercel.app` as the brand reference.

Use the actual website content as the source of truth. Do not invent products, services, prices, or business claims. If the website cannot be accessed, use neutral brand imagery rather than making up details.

## Delivery format

- Duration: 30 seconds
- Format: vertical 9:16
- Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels
- Required qualities: realistic visuals, professional voice-over, clear captions, polished editing, and high-quality sound
- Desired outcome: persuasive and memorable

## Required scene structure

### Scene 1 — 0–5 seconds

Open with an eye-catching cinematic shot, smooth camera movement, and an intriguing introduction to the Sisabena Paano brand.

### Scene 2 — 5–12 seconds

Showcase the website on a modern laptop and smartphone. Show a person browsing the website naturally, with clean close-up shots of the screen.

### Scene 3 — 12–21 seconds

Present the website's actual services, products, or features using attractive visuals that accurately match the website. Show how it benefits customers and makes their experience convenient.

### Scene 4 — 21–26 seconds

Show happy, confident customers engaging with the brand. Use realistic people, professional lighting, smooth transitions, and uplifting background music.

### Scene 5 — 26–30 seconds

End with a beautiful branded closing screen displaying exactly:

“SISABENA PAANO”

“Visit us online today!”

`https://sisabena-paano.vercel.app`
