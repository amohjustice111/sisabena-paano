# Style Guide — Sisabena Paano 30-Second Ad

## Visual direction

- Modern, premium, cinematic, realistic, and visually engaging.
- Prioritise genuine motion, smooth camera movement, professional lighting, natural device interaction, and clean close-ups.
- Keep the website presentation authentic: use readable captures or supplied visual evidence from the actual website, not speculative features or invented product pages.
- Use attractive but restrained transitions so the edit stays polished and does not become motion-graphics-led.
- Frame key device screens and captions for 9:16 mobile safe areas.

## Audio direction

- Use a professional, clear voice-over based only on approved website evidence.
- Provide clear, synchronized captions.
- Use uplifting background music with a premium, contemporary tone. Keep it supportive and quieter than voice-over.
- Avoid lyrics or spoken music that competes with narration.

## Copy and claims

- Website evidence is mandatory for statements about offerings, features, customer benefits, prices, availability, reviews, performance, or business operations.
- Never invent products, services, pricing, guarantees, customer outcomes, or claims.
- The final end screen must display the exact closing text specified in `creative_brief.md`.
- If website access fails, use neutral branded imagery and the approved closing URL only after surfacing the limitation.

## Platform and format

- Deliver in vertical 9:16 at approximately 30 seconds.
- Optimise readability for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels.
